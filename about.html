About

Simulation, rendering, and the graphics in between — with a dog named Leo never far from the work.

I build systems that simulate and render physical behavior — soft bodies, particles, and neural approaches to graphics.

My work sits between computer graphics, HCI, and animal-computer interaction. Off the clock I'm usually with Leo, my dog — who has a habit of turning up in my projects, from gait analysis to the mascot slowly taking over this site. I build on an RTX 4090 laptop and like problems where a good simulation is the whole answer.

I'm finishing my degree and headed into research-focused graduate study, where I want to keep working on how virtual matter behaves and how people — and animals — interact with it.
